MyBoaty Privacy Policy

Operator — See From Afar Vision LLC
Privacy contact — hello@sfa.vision
Effective date — The date MyBoaty is first publicly released on the App Store

MyBoaty is a bedtime Bible story app for families. This policy explains what the app stores on your device, what our purchase service receives, and how parents and guardians can manage their children’s information.

Child profiles and listening

A parent or guardian can create a profile with a child’s first name or nickname, age, gender, and an optional avatar. Please leave out the child’s last name. If you select a photo for the avatar, the app saves a cropped copy on your device.

MyBoaty stores child profiles, shared family listening progress, and bedtime reminder settings on your device. If you allow notifications, your device schedules the reminders. MyBoaty does not sync these records across devices. Apple’s device backups may include locally stored app data, depending on your settings.

The app does not send onboarding steps or listening activity to a separate product analytics service.

Personalized story audio

During setup, MyBoaty explains how it uses a child’s name to personalize story audio. If you tap Get started after seeing that notice and personalization is available, MyBoaty can send the first name or nickname you enter, the story language, and voice settings to our voice service hosted by Cloudflare. The service sends text containing the name, along with the language and voice settings, to Inworld to generate spoken greetings, name mentions, and goodnight messages. We do not send the child’s photo, age, or gender for this feature. MyBoaty does not record the child’s voice.

Our voice service uses Apple App Attest to verify app installations and keeps installation verification records and request counts to prevent abuse. MyBoaty saves generated clips on the device. When a personalized clip is unavailable, stories can use standard greetings.

App Store purchases

Apple handles App Store payments and subscriptions. MyBoaty does not receive your full payment card details.

MyBoaty connects to RevenueCat at startup to load purchase options, even if you make no purchase. We also use RevenueCat to help verify and restore purchases and report on purchases, subscriptions, and refunds. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user ID and, when iOS makes it available, Apple’s device identifier for our apps (IDFV). It also receives technical information such as device model, operating system, app version, and preferred language, as well as purchase and subscription information when available. RevenueCat may use your connection’s IP address to estimate your country if the purchase record does not include it.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can edit or remove a child’s profile under Settings › Children. Removing a profile deletes it and its saved personalized clips from the device. Shared family listening progress stays on the device. Apple manages any existing device backups separately.

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and resolve your request. We keep purchase and support records as needed to manage purchases, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Parents and guardians can email hello@sfa.vision to ask us to review, correct, or delete their child’s information, or to stop collecting or using it. We may ask for enough information to verify the request and locate the relevant records. RevenueCat processes requests to delete customer records and related purchase history asynchronously. Apple manages its own purchase records and subscriptions. Restoring purchases later may create a new RevenueCat record.

We’ll post updates to this policy here with the new effective date.
